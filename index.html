About Echo Arcade

Echo Arcade is a free, ad-supported collection of original browser games. Every game is written from scratch in plain HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, runs on desktop and mobile, and never needs an install or an account.

What is inside

  • Shooters: Bot Arena (you vs. waves of bots) and Squad Clash (4 vs 4 team rounds).
  • Strategy: Summon Siege, a lane-based summoning battle.
  • Story: The Impossible Detective, a 15-chapter locked-room murder mystery with a twist in every chapter.
  • Factory: Deep Forge, a five-layer mining and crafting factory.
  • Daily: Daily Cipher, a code-breaking puzzle that changes every day.
  • Fighting: Button Brawl, where the player who presses the most different buttons wins.
  • Funny mini games: pigeons, toast, cats, cows, chickens and emoji.

Your progress and best scores are saved in your own browser (local storage). See the privacy policy for details.
